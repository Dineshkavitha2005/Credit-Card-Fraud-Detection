Do I need technical skills to use Omrix?

Not at all. If you can drag and drop, you can build a workflow. Omrix is designed for marketers, ops teams, founders, and PMs no code, ever.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every plan comes with a 14-day free trial, full Pro access included. No credit card required to start.

How many tools can I connect?

100+ native integrations out of the box Slack, HubSpot, Stripe, Notion, GitHub, and more. Custom webhooks available on Business and above.

Is my data safe with Omrix?

Completely. We're SOC 2 Type II certified, end-to-end encrypted, and GDPR compliant. Your data is yours always.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes, anytime. Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel directly from your account. No contracts, no lock-ins, no penalties.

What kind of support do I get?

Starter gets community support. Pro gets priority email. Business gets a dedicated onboarding specialist. We're here at every stage.
